Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 22, 2026

FunnyDigits is a fun educational app designed to help children learn to write numbers using interactive guidance and secure, offline pattern recognition.

1. Data Collection & User Control

We deeply respect user privacy. By default, we do not collect, automatically store, or track any personal information, names, emails, locations, or drawing data. All standard drawing practices and number recognitions are processed strictly offline and locally on your device.

2. User-Initiated Feedback (Optional Feature)

Our app includes an optional, premium feedback mechanism to help improve our local recognition accuracy. Only when a user manually objects to a prediction and explicitly chooses to send feedback, the single image of that specific drawn digit is securely transmitted to our Firebase servers. No drawing is ever saved or sent without the user's direct, manual interaction and consent. These images are stored anonymously and used solely to optimize our model's accuracy.

3. Children's Privacy & COPPA Compliance

The app is strictly designed for children under 13 and fully complies with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and GDPR-K regulations. Since data transmission only occurs via explicit, manual user action (intended for parents/guardians managing the app), we do not knowingly or automatically harvest data from children.

4. Infrastructure

The app runs securely on Google Firebase servers to ensure a safe environment for all optional cloud features.
