FunnyDigits Legal Information

Last updated: July 31, 2026

Privacy Policy

FunnyDigits is an educational app designed for children to learn numbers. We respect user privacy deeply. We do not automatically collect, track, or store personal information (names, emails, or locations). Digit drawing data is processed locally on your device.

Optional Feedback: If a user manually reports an issue, a single image of the drawn digit may be sent to our Firebase servers for model improvement. This is strictly user-initiated, anonymized, and requires explicit consent.

Data Retention & Deletion Policy

In accordance with data minimization principles, anonymized operational logs and reports generated to improve app performance and AI accuracy are automatically deleted after specified periods using secure server mechanisms (such as Firestore TTL):

  • Prediction Logs: Stored for a maximum of 90 days.
  • Daily Reports: Retained for 30 days.
  • Weekly Reports: Retained for 90 days.
  • Stats Summary: Archived for up to 12 months.

Compliance: The app is designed for children under 13 and fully complies with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and GDPR-K regulations. No personal data is harvested from children.

Terms of Use

By using FunnyDigits, you agree to the following terms:

  • Data Usage: You acknowledge that anonymized, user-submitted data may be used to improve our AI models.
  • Content Standards: Users are prohibited from using the app to create or share malicious, inappropriate, or harmful content.
  • Infrastructure: The app utilizes Google Firebase for secure cloud services and maintenance of a safe learning environment.
